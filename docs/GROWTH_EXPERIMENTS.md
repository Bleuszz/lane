## Experiment record template

ID, owner, date/build, hypothesis, eligible ICP, permission/approval, landing/creative ID, one changed variable, cohort/source, expected mechanism, all-in cap, observation window, numerator/denominator, exclusions, actual costs, outcome, limitations, next decision. Publish only aggregate results in Git. No pilot identities, cookie data or billing credentials.

Separate **learning**, **activation** and **profitability** decisions. A useful conversation can justify another organic experiment; it does not justify raising an ad budget. A cheap trial with no return use is not a winning campaign. A high conversion percentage from one friend is not sufficient to scale.

## Kill and reassessment protocol

- Immediately pause promotion affected by broken auth, unsafe behaviour, false product claim, bad installer link, lost attribution or marketplace challenge. No unsupported action workarounds.
- If £5 yields no qualified signup, hold the next £10 and diagnose voluntarily with organic testers. At £10 cumulative no signup, stop paid; at £15 no activation, stop paid. Small samples do not disprove demand; these limits protect a tiny cash reserve.
- If >20% of **observable** search clicks are clearly irrelevant, pause and fix match/negative/landing alignment; with fewer than ten observable terms treat the ratio as provisional and act on obvious mismatch rather than claiming significance.
- If landing traffic seems relevant but click→signup <5% after at least 40 attributable clicks, investigate clarity/trust/device friction. This is a working trigger chosen around the mid-case assumption, not an industry standard; £30 may never reach that sample.
- After two experiments fail at the same gate without new evidence, stop repeating and inspect the actual user journey. Preserve the remaining budget.
- Scaling gates and CAC calculations are exclusively in [MARKETING_BUDGET.md](MARKETING_BUDGET.md); do not substitute a platform's optimisation recommendation.

## Micro-creator selection, later execution

Research three public UK clothing-reseller creators whose recent posts show real Vinted/eBay work and genuine questions from sellers. Subscriber count is not the decision variable. Ask for audience geography/device fit only if a conversation is requested/permissioned. No scraped contact list. Offer the existing pilot, not payment, affiliate revenue or an unimplemented reward. They retain editorial control; any benefit must be disclosed. Measure qualified source signups and activation rather than views. If all require paid sponsorship, defer, keep £30 and use own demos.
