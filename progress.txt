IMPLEMENTED
- Redesigned landing page, app palette, help centre, billing and accessible forms.
- Destination review screen with selected photos, fields, pricing rules and blockers.
- Mobile inventory cards, compact header, modal navigation/focus restoration and
  scroll-safe preview. Bulk actions require explicit selection; shortcuts ignore controls.
- Saved drafts can be previewed before connecting an account; actual publish stays gated.
- Bulk publish reviews every saved item and requires explicit final batch confirmation.
  Changed content/prices/destinations invalidate approval; queue writes are transactional.
- Queued content actions reference immutable owner-scoped listing snapshots. Retries
  retain text, photos, fields and price; live stock can only reduce queued quantity.
- Database-generated partial-sale jobs use quantity-only eBay updates, preserving content.
- New sales retain separate durable follow-ups even during active content/stock jobs.
  Duplicate sale delivery still cannot decrement stock twice; channel leases serialize jobs.
- Temporary eBay connection/read failures and HTTP 408/429/500/502/503/504 receive
  bounded exponential retry with jitter, provider Retry-After and a five-attempt ceiling.
  Activity exposes cooldown; invalid data/auth/unknown errors remain reviewable.
- Stock and account availability are rechecked before each eBay photo/content/quantity
  write. A reduction stops stale work; retry loads current stock with approved content.
  Successful completion preserves a concurrent account pause and records updated quantity.
- eBay photo delivery uploads local JPEG/PNG copies through its Media API on an
  authorized job. Cached receipts preserve order, survive partial failures and expire;
  originals and queued snapshots stay unchanged. No separate storage service added.
- Local Photo studio: rotate, framing, lighting/contrast, 1200px JPEG copy; original retained.
- Source normalization preserves photos, category identity, UK size ranges, colours,
  brand and unknown condition/material; neutral per-marketplace field overrides.
- Atomic item/photo/tag creation; transactional source imports and item edits.
- Durable job intent deduplication, atomic claims/leases, guarded retries, eBay
  offer reconciliation, sale-event deduplication and transactional delist outbox.
- Seven-day no-card trial: 25 publish/relist actions, zero AI credits.
- Optional AI extraction with evidence/allowed-value checks, manual override protection,
  credit ledger, usage estimates, refunds and monthly provider-cost guards.
- First-crosslist checklist and first-occurrence activation events.
- Per-seller eBay policy/location selection, verified against that account on save.
- Dedicated-secret worker endpoint, bounded batches; no scheduler activated.
- Paused/unverified accounts cannot claim jobs.
- Proposed GBP9/19/29 plans; checkout gated until pricing/billing is verified.
- Signed billing webhooks: durable deduplication, serialized current-subscription reads,
  paid-invoice access checks, owner binding, retryable rollback and unknown-price denial.
- Builds no longer automatically migrate a database. Windows scripts are portable.
